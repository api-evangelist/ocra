Mauritius has tightened its beneficial ownership (BO) and ultimate beneficial ownership (UBO) regulations. Through the Finance Act 2025, several amendments were made to the Companies Act 2001, elevating UBO disclosure from a routine administrative duty to a foundational pillar of the country’s Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Combatting the Financing of Terrorism (CFT) compliance framework.
